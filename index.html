About Me

"Hi, Namaste! I am Brp Mailo. A literary figure, teacher, and web designer from Nepal. Click here to read more about me!"

I am Brp Mailo. I am a literary figure, teacher, and web designer from Nepal. I have a deep passion for preserving the Nepali language and actively work in literature, especially in Ghazals and novels. I am currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Nepali literature in the Faculty of Education at Tribhuvan University. Along with my studies, I also work as a social teacher and computer operator at Lovely Angels English Boarding School. Besides teaching, I have created a website (baburamphuyal.com.np) where I provide notes, curriculum, teacher guides, and literary content for students. I am also a skilled web designer and have designed and sold over 10 web templates. Apart from literature and teaching, I run a YouTube channel called "Hamro Computer Guru," where I teach computer-related topics. I am currently working on designing a book for my Ghazals. I am well-liked by the youth for my contributions to Nepali literature. My Ghazals are often inspired by my personal experiences, including past relationships, though I take them in a fun and lighthearted way. I strongly believe in discipline and integrity, especially in education, and I do not support cheating in exams. Currently, I am also focused on creating videos covering all Excel commands.
